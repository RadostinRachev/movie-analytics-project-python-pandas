## Movie Analytics Project

## Overview

This project performs exploratory data analysis (EDA) on a movie dataset to investigate the relationship between movie genres, popularity, ratings, budget, revenue, and profit.

The objective of the analysis is to identify patterns that contribute to a movie's commercial and audience success using statistical analysis and data visualizations.

## Research Questions

Which genres are the most common based on the number of movies produced?

Which genres have the highest average budget and revenue?

Which genres generate the highest average profit?

Which genres have the highest average popularity?

Which genres contain the highest number of highly rated movies (vote average ≥ 8)?
## Research Hypotheses

Movies with higher vote averages generate higher profit and revenue.

Movies with higher popularity generate higher profit and revenue.

High-budget movies generate higher revenue and profit.

High-budget movies tend to have higher popularity.

## Key Findings

Drama and Comedy were the most common movie genres in the dataset.

Adventure movies generated the highest average revenue, profit, and popularity.

Fantasy, Science Fiction, and Action movies also showed strong financial performance.

Higher movie budgets were positively associated with higher revenue, profit, and popularity.

Movies with higher popularity and vote averages generally achieved better financial results.

Documentary and Music genres achieved some of the highest average ratings despite lower profitability.

The analysis supported most of the initial research hypotheses regarding budget, popularity, ratings, and movie success.
